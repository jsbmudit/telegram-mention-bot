RAID BOT COMMANDS
=================

Bot: @raids_bot

Command names are picked so they don't clash with Rose or other group bots.
That's why it's /raidlock instead of /lock, /greet instead of /welcome,
and /raidhelp instead of /help.


FOR EVERYONE
------------

/register
    Link your X account and Verona address so your raid comments get counted.
    Typed in the group, it gives you a button that opens a private chat with the bot.
    The button disappears as soon as you tap it, or after 15 seconds.
    The bot asks two things:
      1. Your X username or profile link (@name, name or x.com/name all work)
      2. Your Verona address (starts with xion1)
    Then you press Confirm. Run it again any time to change your details.

/me
    (private chat with the bot) Shows the X account and Verona address you registered.

/leaderboard
    This week's leaderboard for the group.
    Points: 1 per raid you comment on, +1 extra if you comment within 10 minutes.
    Streak: how many raids in a row you've joined.
    Weeks run Monday to Sunday (UTC).

/raidhelp
    Shows the list of commands.


FOR ADMINS: TAGGING AND RAIDS
-----------------------------

@all
    Tags everyone in the group, 5 people per message.
    Registered members are tagged first. Unregistered members are tagged after,
    with a reminder to /register.
    Can be used once every 15 minutes (shared with @raidall).
    The tag messages delete themselves after 4 hours.
    /all does the same thing.

@raidall
@raidall fun
    Same tagging as @all, then posts a raid template with the X link filled in
    and pins it with a notification to everyone.
    @raidall on its own uses the default template. @raidall fun uses the
    template called fun (see /settemplate below).
    The link is taken from, in this order:
      1. the same message (example: @raidall fun https://x.com/name/status/123)
      2. the message you're replying to
      3. the last X link you posted in the group (within 24 hours)
    A raid lasts 12 hours. While it runs, the bot checks the post's replies
    every minute and counts comments from registered members only.
    @allraid, /raidall and /allraid do the same thing.

/endraid
    Ends a raid early and posts the summary. Any "Chat unlocked" summary from
    that raid is deleted then, so only the final summary stays.
    If more than one raid is running, the bot sends you buttons privately so you
    can pick which one.

/raidlock 15 60m
    Locks the chat for everyone except admins until 15 registered members have
    commented on the latest raid, or until 60 minutes pass, whichever comes first.
    Pick any numbers, for example /raidlock 10 30m, /raidlock 20 2h, /raidlock 30 90m.
    Only works while a raid is running. The bot checks for comments every
    minute. The group's normal permissions come back exactly as they were.

/raidunlock
    Unlocks the chat early.


FOR ADMINS: X FEED
------------------
Posts from any X account show up in the group automatically. Needs the X API key.

/feedon VeronaOfficial
    Starts posting that account's new X posts in this group. You can also use
    @VeronaOfficial or the profile link. It starts from now, so old posts
    aren't dumped in. The bot checks every minute. Posts with an image are
    sent with the image. Replies and reposts are left out.

/feedon VeronaOfficial all
    Same, but also includes their replies and reposts.

/feedon VeronaOfficial pin
    Same as /feedon VeronaOfficial, and every new post is also pinned with a
    notification to everyone. Without pin, posts are never pinned.
    You can combine it with all: /feedon VeronaOfficial all pin
    Running /feedon again for the same account replaces its settings, so
    /feedon VeronaOfficial on its own turns pinning off again.

/feedoff VeronaOfficial
    Stops it.

/feeds
    Lists the X feeds that are on in this group, and which ones pin their posts.

    You can have as many feeds as you want, in as many groups as you want.
    Feed posts stay in the chat (they aren't auto-deleted).


FOR ADMINS: SETUP
-----------------

/addusers @name1 @name2 @name3
    Adds people to the tag list. Only needed once, when the bot is new.
    After that, people are added automatically when they send a message or join,
    and removed when they leave.

/removeuser @name
    Takes someone off the tag list.

/members
    Shows how many members the bot knows, and who isn't registered yet.

/settemplate
/settemplate fun
    Saves a raid template. Without a name it's the default template; with a name
    (fun, bull, anything with letters, numbers or _) you get a named one.
    Type it in the group. The bot then asks for the template in your private
    chat with it, and you send it there:
      - send plain text for a text-only template, or
      - send a photo with the template as its caption, and that image is
        attached every time this template is used.
    Running it again with the same name replaces that template.
    You can use these in the text:
      {link}      the X post link (required)
      {xname}     the X display name of whoever posted it, e.g. Andyyy
      {xhandle}   their @handle, e.g. @andyyy
      {xprofile}  a link to their X profile
    Example:
        Our dear friend {xname} is posting, let's show him how Verona bulls are!
        {link}
        Follow: {xprofile}
    {xname} works even without the X API key.

/templates
    Lists every template and how to use it.

/template fun
    Shows a preview of a template (with an example post by Andyyy).
    /template on its own previews the default one.

/deltemplate fun
    Deletes a template.

/setgreet
    Sets the welcome message for new members. Type it in the group, and the bot
    asks you to send the message in your private chat with it.
    Put {name} where the new member's name should go. A Register button is added
    automatically.

/greet
    Posts the welcome message. Reply to someone's message with /greet to
    welcome that person.
    The welcome message deletes itself once that person registers, or after 24 hours.

/cancel
    Stops /settemplate, /setgreet or /register halfway through.


FOR ADMINS: REPORTS
-------------------
Reports are sent to you in a private chat with the bot, never in the group.
Press Start on @raids_bot once so it can message you.

/raidcsv
    CSV of the latest raid.

/raidcsv 3
    CSV of raid number 3 (raid numbers are shown in each raid summary).

/raidcsv all
    CSV of every raid in one file.

    The raid number, raid link and start time are listed once at the top.
    Below that is one row per member with these columns: Telegram username,
    X profile link, Verona address, registered, raided, reply link,
    reply time, time after raid start.
    People with no Telegram username show their Telegram name instead.
    With /raidcsv all, each raid gets its own block, with an empty row between.

Weekly leaderboard
    Posted in the group automatically every Monday (UTC).
    Every admin who has pressed Start on the bot also gets a private CSV of the
    top 5, with their X profile links and Verona addresses, for sending rewards.


KEEPING THE CHAT CLEAN
----------------------
- Every bot command (/addusers, /raidlock, /raidunlock, /raidcsv and so on) is deleted
  from the group about 3 seconds after it's sent.
- "@all" or "@raidall fun <link>" sent on their own are deleted the same way.
  If you wrote a real message around them ("@all gm fam, call in 10"), your
  message stays.
- Anything only an admin needs is sent to that admin in a private chat, not the
  group: confirmations (like "X feed on"), errors, usage hints, cooldown notes,
  /feeds, /templates, /members, template previews, the /settemplate and
  /setgreet setup, the /endraid buttons, and problems posting or pinning a raid.
  Each one starts with the group's name, so you know which group it's about.
- For that to work, press Start on @raids_bot once. If you haven't, or you post
  as an anonymous admin, the answer shows in the group instead with a note, and
  deletes after 15 seconds (1 minute for lists and previews). /settemplate and
  /setgreet only work in the private chat, so they just show the note.
- /raidhelp and /leaderboard are for everyone, so they stay in the group and
  delete after 1 minute.
- The "Chat locked" notice is removed when the chat unlocks.
- The "Chat unlocked" summary is removed when the raid ends and its final
  summary is posted.
- Tag messages delete after 4 hours. Welcome messages delete when the person
  registers, or after 24 hours.
- What stays: the pinned raid message, the final raid summary, and the weekly
  leaderboard.


AUTOMATIC THINGS
----------------
- Raid summary when a raid ends: how many commented, the fastest, and who missed it.
- Lock summary when a lock ends, whether the target was reached, time ran out or an admin unlocked.
  It stays until the raid ends, then the final raid summary replaces it.
- Welcome message for new members, with a Register button.
- New members are added to the tag list automatically, and people who leave are removed.
